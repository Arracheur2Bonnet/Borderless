The idea

The model never touches the money.

The AI reads your request. Fixed rules and your approval move the money.

Can be fooled

The model proposes

  • Writes a request, nothing more. No tools. It never sees a saved account number.
  • Rules, a local Qwen model or any connected model: swappable.
  • Smart cascade: rules first, Qwen only when needed.
  • Grounding check: every field must appear in what you wrote.
Fixed rules

Code decides

  • Account numbers come only from her saved payees.
  • The planner simulates the whole plan first.
  • 14 named rules give a risk level, R0 to R4.
You and the ledger

Lina approves, the ledger pays

  • One tap or a one-time code, in proportion to the risk.
  • Locked to a hash of the exact payment.
  • Every action gets a linked receipt. Lina keeps a signed copy of the latest.

A fooled model can only propose.

Why it holds

Only one of eight controls reads the text.

1

Content guard

Finds hidden instructions in documents. It only warns.

Reads the text
2

Registry-only account numbers

No account number from a document or chat is ever paid.

3

Approval locked to a hash

Change the payment and the approval is refused. A replay never pays twice.

4

Codes lock

3 wrong codes freeze every approval for the user.

5

Payee name check

Name compared with the bank's record. A mismatch needs a code. A swapped invoice account is denied.

6

Customers separated

Other customers' data is unreachable.

7

Split payments

Same payee, over 1,500 EUR in 24 h: a code is needed.

Financial crime
8

Sanctions screen

Watchlist names and accounts, look-alike letters folded: denied.

The policy

14 named rules on every payment.
R4 has no override.

R0
Read-only
Allowed.
R2
One tap

R3
One-time code
New payee, payee name mismatch, over 1,500 EUR, exchange over 2,500 EUR, split payments.
R4
Denied. No override.
Unregistered or blocked payee, account number not in the registry, another customer, reserve floor, over 4,000 per payment or 5,000 per day, untrusted origin, sanctioned, invoice account with the wrong name or no bank record.

Honest limits

What it does not do yet.

  • A sandbox. The ledger, the exchange rates and the SMS code are simulated.
  • The offline parser is English only and misses unusual phrasing: 24 of 30 blind.
  • One model family measured blind (Qwen 7B and 3B). A connected frontier model is not tested yet.
  • The content guard is rule-based, and it only warns.
  • One student, USD and EUR, a public demo signing key. Credit building and recurring payments are not covered.

We claim a safe architecture, measured blind.

We do not claim production readiness.

Appendix · architecture

How a request flows.

Left of the line, a model can be fooled. Right of it, plain code: no model, no network call.

Untrusted input

Her message, the invoice or email, the exchange-rate reply. Documents are data, never orders.

Model proposes

Parser: rules, local Qwen, cascade or a connected model. No tools. It sees only her text and her payee names: no saved IBAN, no balance.

Trusted: deterministic code
