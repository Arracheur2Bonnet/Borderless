The idea

The model never touches the money.

The AI reads your request. Fixed rules and your approval move the money.

Can be fooled

The model proposes

  • Writes a request, nothing more. No tools. It never sees an account number.
  • Rules, a local Qwen model or any connected model: swappable.
  • Grounding check: every field must appear in what you wrote.
Fixed rules

Code decides

  • Account numbers come only from her saved payees.
  • The planner simulates the whole plan first.
  • 13 named rules give a risk level, R0 to R4.
You and the ledger

Lina approves, the ledger pays

  • One tap or a one-time code, in proportion to the risk.
  • Locked to a hash of the exact payment.
  • Every action gets a linked receipt. Lina keeps a signed copy of the latest.

A fooled model can only propose.
